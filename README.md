## 📌 About The Training

This repository documents a **10-Day Beyond Curriculum Training (BCT)** on **Very Large Scale Integration (VLSI)** conducted for undergraduate students.

It includes all **presentations, notes, and supporting materials** used throughout the program, aimed at building a strong foundation in **chip design and semiconductor technology**.

---

## 🎯 Training Objectives

✨ Understand the **VLSI Design Flow**  
✨ Learn **CMOS Technology & MOSFET Basics**  
✨ Explore **Digital Circuit Design**  
✨ Get introduced to **HDL (Verilog/VHDL)**  
✨ Gain exposure to **EDA Tools & Simulation**
